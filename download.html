Download

Phosphor comes in two parts: a plugin for your Jellyfin server, and an app for your Roku. You need both.

Phosphor isn't out yet. It's in its last round of testing. This page will have the links on release day.

1. The Jellyfin plugin

For Jellyfin 10.11 or later. You'll install it from the Plugins page in Jellyfin's dashboard, and Jellyfin will tell you when there's an update.

Coming soon

Once it's installed, restart Jellyfin. Phosphor then shows up in the dashboard menu. That's where you build your channels.

2. The Roku app

Free, from the Roku Streaming Store.

Coming soon

  1. On your Roku (or in the Roku mobile app), open the Streaming Store and search for Phosphor.
  2. Choose Add app.
  3. Open Phosphor. It looks for your Jellyfin server on your home network. If it can't find it, type the address shown on the Help tab of Phosphor's setup page.
  4. Sign in with a Quick Connect code, or your Jellyfin username and password.
