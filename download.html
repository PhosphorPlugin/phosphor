Download

Phosphor has two parts: a plugin on your Jellyfin server, and an app on your Roku.

1. The Jellyfin plugin

Works with Jellyfin 10.11 and 12. You'll add Phosphor to your server from Jellyfin's own plugin catalog, and Jellyfin will install the right version and let you know about updates.

Coming soon

2. The Roku app

Phosphor for Roku is free in the Roku Channel Store.

Coming soon to the Roku Channel Store

  1. On your Roku, open Streaming Store (or the Roku mobile app) and search for Phosphor.
  2. Choose Add app.
  3. Open Phosphor. It finds your Jellyfin server on your home network by itself. If it doesn't, type the address the plugin shows under Help.
