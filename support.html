Support

Answers to common questions about the Phosphor Roku app and plugin

Still stuck? Email phosphorplugin@proton.me. Tell us what happened, what you expected, and which channel you were on. We usually reply within a few days.

Getting started

The Roku app can't find my server

The app looks for Jellyfin servers on your home network by itself. If it doesn't find yours, type your server's address on the sign-in screen, for example http://192.168.1.20:8096. The Help tab on Phosphor's setup page shows the exact address to use. Your Roku and your server need to be on the same home network.

I don't see any channels

Channels are made on your computer, not on the TV. Open Jellyfin's dashboard, choose Phosphor in the menu, and either let Autobuild make a lineup for you or create channels one at a time. Press Save, and the channels show up on your TVs.

Where do the commercials come from?

Phosphor doesn't come with any. Upload your own clips on the Clips tab of the setup page: commercials, station IDs, bumpers, and intros for movies or blocks. Phosphor keeps them in a "Phosphor Clips" library that's hidden from Jellyfin's home screens. Without station clips, Phosphor shows simple title cards for station IDs and intros.

Watching

A show won't play, or takes a long time to start

Most files play right away. Some formats have to be converted by your Jellyfin server first, which can take several seconds. Turning on hardware acceleration in Jellyfin's Playback settings helps.

A show plays in the wrong language

Phosphor uses the audio language from your Jellyfin profile. In Jellyfin, go to Settings → Playback and set Preferred audio language, for example to English. If a file doesn't have that language, its default track plays.

Will Phosphor mark my shows as watched?

No. Nothing you see on Phosphor changes your watch history or your "Continue watching" list in Jellyfin.

The weather channel is empty

Pick your location on the Weather tab of the setup page. Weather works for locations in the United States only.

Parental controls

I forgot the PIN

Open the setup page on your computer, type a new PIN and press Save. The new PIN replaces the old one on every TV.

How do I hide a channel?

Turn it off, either on the setup page or from the guide on your TV. A channel that's off stays hidden until you turn it back on.
