Support

Help with the Phosphor Roku app and plugin

Email phosphorplugin@proton.me with what happened, what you expected, and which channel you were on. We usually reply within a few days.

The Roku app can't find my server

The app looks for Jellyfin servers on your home network by itself. If it doesn't find yours, type your server's address on the setup screen, for example http://192.168.1.20:8096. The Phosphor plugin's setup page shows the address to use under Help.

I don't see any channels

Channels come from the Phosphor plugin on your Jellyfin server. Open Jellyfin's dashboard on your computer, go to Plugins and then Phosphor, and create channels (or use Autobuild). Press Save, and the channels appear on your TVs.

A show won't play, or takes a long time to start

Most files play directly. Some formats have to be converted by your Jellyfin server first, which can take several seconds, especially without hardware acceleration turned on in Jellyfin's playback settings.

The weather channel is empty

Pick your location on the plugin's Weather page. Weather is available for locations in the United States.
