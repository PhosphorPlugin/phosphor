Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

Phosphor is a Roku app and a Jellyfin server plugin. In short: we don't collect, store, sell or share any of your information. Phosphor talks to your own Jellyfin server, and your server is yours.

What the Roku app stores

To stay connected, the app saves your Jellyfin server's address and the sign-in token Jellyfin gives it, on your Roku device. It also remembers small settings on the device, like your favorite channels, reminders and recently shown commercials. This information stays on your Roku. You can remove it by signing out in the app or removing the app.

Where your information goes

The app only connects to the Jellyfin server you choose. Your username and password are sent to that server to sign in, the same way any Jellyfin app works. Phosphor's developer never receives them.

The app does not include analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting from us or anyone else.

What the plugin stores

The Phosphor plugin runs on your Jellyfin server and keeps its settings there, including your channels and a short list of the TVs that use it (each TV's device ID, its name and when it was last seen). A TV that hasn't been seen for 90 days is removed from the list. A parental PIN is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form. None of this leaves your server.

The plugin and outside services

If you set up the weather channel, your server requests forecasts, alerts and maps for the location you picked from these public services:

  • The U.S. National Weather Service (api.weather.gov and mapservices.weather.noaa.gov)
  • OpenStreetMap (map tiles)
  • Open-Meteo (to look up the place you type in)

These requests come from your server and include only the location you chose, not who you are. Those services have their own privacy policies. If you don't use the weather channel, the plugin doesn't contact them.

Roku

Roku collects information about how its devices and apps are used, under Roku's own privacy policy. Phosphor doesn't receive that information.

Children

Phosphor isn't directed to children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone. What plays is whatever is on your own server, and you can lock channels or limit ratings in the plugin.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Questions: phosphorplugin@proton.me.
